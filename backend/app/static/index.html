Live analytics and behavioral biometrics

    
Live observations evaluate analytic rules, baselines, and library rules on arrival. The library contains 170 behavioral measures and 328 prebuilt rules, including first-party definitions over 80 signals. A measure with no observations has missing coverage.

    
Biometric signals compare aggregate typing and mouse movement with a trusted baseline. A consistent sample supports review of identity; a deviation may reflect device, accessibility or task changes. It does not prove identity, account takeover or credential sharing.
